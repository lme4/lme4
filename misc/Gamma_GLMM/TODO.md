* GH #936 log-gaussian `sigma` bias: mechanism precisely characterized (glmer's moment-based phi is missing a degrees-of-freedom correction) and now **implemented and generalized**: `glmerControl(disp_dof_correction=TRUE)` (opt-in, default `FALSE`), wired through `mkGlmerDevfun()`/`mkdevfun()`/`glmerPwrssUpdate()`/`glmerLaplaceHandle()` down to `profilePhi()` in `external.cpp`. `qEff` is computed once per fit (doesn't depend on theta/phi/beta) in R (`computeQEff()`, `R/modular.R`) as the actual rank of the combined fixed+random "saturated" design `[X, Z]`, via `Matrix::rankMatrix(cbind(X, t(Zt)), method="qr")` -- the classical `RSS/(n-p)` logic, generalized. (History: started as a naive closed-form guess `q_eff=sum(q_k)-(K-1)`, validated on crossed scalar-intercept terms and a single random-slope term across gaussian/Gamma; then found wrong for nested terms and multiple random-slope terms sharing a covariate (see README); briefly patched with a hand-coded structural gate (`reformulas::isNested()` + a shared-slope-covariate check) before being replaced entirely by the general rank computation, which reproduces every case checked so far -- including the two the naive formula got wrong -- without needing failure modes enumerated by hand.) Verified the rank computation itself is cheap relative to fitting (`method="qr"` avoids `rankMatrix()`'s default `svd()`-based densification of sparse input; confirmed negligible cost even at `q=1000`). **Not yet done**: regression tests for `disp_dof_correction`/`computeQEff()`, a NEWS item, and validation on real (not just simulated) data beyond the `sleepstudy` spot-checks
